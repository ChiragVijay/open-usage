Existing provider identity colors: restrained blue ChatGPT #337EA9, muted orange Claude #D9730D, muted purple Cursor #9065B0. Amber only for low allowance. Clear 16px body labels, smaller readable secondary text; ample whitespace, 8px card radius, no heavy shadows, no gradients.
